# Editorial covers

Both original illustrations were generated for this portfolio with OpenAI's built-in image generation tool on October 22, 2026. They are editorial artwork, not documentary photographs. No third-party reference images were supplied. The served WebP files are quality-88 encodings of the generated 1672 × 941 PNGs.

The matching `*-share.jpg` files are 1200 × 675 RGB, quality-90 JPEG exports of those WebPs for social link cards. No additional artwork or text is introduced. The build's Open Graph and Twitter metadata reference these images; on-page covers remain WebP.

## jackson-hole-dawn.webp

Prompt: Refined panoramic 16:9 editorial illustration of the Grand Tetons from Jackson Lake at first light. Layered slate-blue mountain silhouettes above a quiet lake; muted gold dawn grazing the ridge. Broad graphic forms, lithographic grain, subtle ink texture, navy foreground and warm pale-gold sky. No people, buildings, financial icons, charts, lettering, logo, border, or watermark. Avoid stock photography, clip art, cartoon, neon, and glossy 3D.

## ai-information-apertures.webp

Prompt: Sophisticated panoramic 16:9 conceptual magazine illustration about knowledge, agency, and information control. Interlocking cobalt architectural planes and apertures, coral and ivory passageways, asymmetric diagonal flow, large clear shapes, matte ink and lithographic texture. Deep navy shadows and restrained periwinkle. No text, labels, people, eyes, brains, robots, circuits, chips, network dots, sci-fi effects, grids, or arrows.
